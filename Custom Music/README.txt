Put your own music here.

- Replacements\  songs that replace game songs, named like BGM2_012.ogg (Options > Custom Music > Use replacement tracks)
- Entrances\     songs you can pick as a wrestler's entrance music (Launcher > Mods > Custom entrances)
- Menus\         every song here (any depth) joins the menu music playlist

ogg, mp3, flac, wav work. Your files stay on your PC (they are not part of the GitHub repository).
